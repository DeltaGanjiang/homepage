All marks belong to their respective institutions. Tsinghua, NUS, and USP vectors are retained without rasterization to stay sharp at any display scale; Shanghai Jiao Tong University uses a 316 px seal rather than its small favicon. Oxford and UCL icons were downloaded from a public favicon cache because their main websites rejected automated asset downloads. No visitor requests are sent to the cache service.
