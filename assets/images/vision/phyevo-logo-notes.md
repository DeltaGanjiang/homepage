# PhyEvo AI identity

Brand: 物演AI / PhyEvo AI. GanJiang is its characterization agent; the homepage remains GanJiang's product homepage.

## Current graphic mark

An angular crystal unfolds into a flowing surface. The angular origin represents matter and physical structure; the opening curves represent continuous evolution. The mark contains no letterforms. Its two jade tones work on light and dark backgrounds.

Final assets: `../phyevo-mark.svg` (symbol) and `../phyevo-logo.svg` (symbol and wordmark). The homepage displays the symbol at 76 CSS pixels, with the PhyEvo AI wordmark below it.

The built-in ImageGen concept is saved as `phyevo-graphic-concept-v3.png`. The final SVGs interpret that silhouette as clean geometric paths, without raster cutout fringes. The earlier P/E monogram concept is superseded.
