The mark joins **P (Physics)** with **E (Evolution)** on a common stem. The precise P counter represents physical structure; the emerging E bars express progressive discovery. The final SVGs are `../phyevo-mark.svg` and `../phyevo-logo.svg`.

## ImageGen concept prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: a finished standalone transparent-background logo symbol for PhyEvo AI, a frontier scientific AI brand whose name means Physics + Evolution. Primary request: design an exceptionally elegant, distinctive, restrained P/E monogram that is CLEARLY RECOGNIZABLE AS THE LETTERS P AND E, not a decorative crystal or abstract ribbon. Meaning: the closed precise P represents physical structure and scientific laws; the E emerges from its shared stem as three clean subtly progressive horizontal elements, representing evolution and generative discovery. Both letters must remain legible. One coherent compact typographic symbol, expert Swiss identity-design quality, excellent optical balance, deliberate negative space, powerful silhouette at 32 pixels. Bold custom geometry with lightly softened corners, flat vector-like finish. Use a single deep jade green #3EAA8E color, optionally one small brighter jade plane for evolution. Transparent background. No wordmark text, no additional letters beyond the P/E monogram, no slogans, no mockup, no gradients, no 3D, no shadow, no atom orbits, no hexagonal badges, no generic AI brain, no enclosing circle, no arrows, no ribbons, no watermark. Center the mark in a square composition with modest clear space. Deliver one single polished mark, not a presentation board or a contact sheet.
